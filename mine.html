How the rig works

4 steps

Arm the rig

Connect any BNB Chain wallet. The rig picks a block window between 5 and 10 minutes for you, differently every session, so no two runs line up.

Wait for the hit

The console streams nonces against the target. When the hash matches, the block is claimed and your bot is assembled.

Mint it

One transaction: 0.001 BNB goes to the project wallet and the bot is minted to you in the same call. Payment and delivery are atomic.

Burn a bot, claim OREBOT

one-time redeem

Check a bot

Enter a token id you own. The rig reads the tier onchain and shows what burning it is worth.

One burn, one claim

The NFT is destroyed in the burn transaction, so the same bot can never be redeemed twice. Payout is issued from the redeem record once the OREBOT reward distributor is live.
