Where the fee goes

One fee per trade, paid in WETH, split inside the swap. Nobody has to sell the coin to get paid. The exact integrator cut is being measured on-chain.

StepWhat happensSet by
1Protocol fee leaves the swap for the AirlockDoppler
2Integrator share accrues to OpenPad, claimable in WETHDoppler constant
3Launcher share accrues to the creator walletLaunch preset

Measured on the first real trade (2.22 FEEBOT bought for 0.00001 ETH): the Airlock recorded 0.000000 WETH of integrator fees for this pool, while protocol fees stayed unchanged. So during the auction the integrator cut is not yet accruing. We will re-check when the auction matures on 2026-11-27 and publish the number with the transaction that proves it.

How a launch works

Pick a preset

Supply, auction range, fee and vesting are fixed before signing and immutable after.

Sign one transaction

Your wallet calls Doppler's create(). Token, auction and pool appear together.

Trade the curve

Price descends from the top of the range until buyers clear it or the auction ends.

Fees accrue

Each swap pays the integrator and the launcher in WETH. Claim any time, no lockup.
